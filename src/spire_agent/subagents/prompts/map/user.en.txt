# RULES
- M is Combat, ? is Unknown (Event/Combat/Shop), E is Elite, E* is Burning Elite, R is Rest, T is Chest, and $ is Shop.
- Count an Unknown room as combat risk, not as a guaranteed safe event. Future rewards, purchases, and healing have not happened yet.
- The current HP, deck, relics, and potions must survive every forced room before a Rest. A later Rest does not prove that the route is currently safe.
- When campfire_rest_healing_available is false, the Rest action cannot recover HP. Coffee Dripper blocks Rest but does not block Eternal Feather; use only the explicit rest_site_healing_sources. If that list is empty, evaluate forced fights after R with the current HP.
- When campfire_smithing_available is false, Rest sites provide no card upgrades. In particular, Fusion Hammer makes R useful only for remaining actions such as Rest or Recall; do not count future R nodes as deck growth.
- If Rest healing and smithing are both unavailable, value R only for explicitly listed relic triggers and remaining actions such as Recall; without a rest_site_healing_source it is not a recovery or upgrade checkpoint.
- In Act 1, value Elite card and relic growth when survivable. In Act 2, increase the risk assigned to hallway fights and Elites. In Act 3, preserve any remaining key opportunity.
- DETERMINISTIC ROUTE EVIDENCE is produced before this call; only its listed options are legal. Encounter readiness applies to one fight only and cannot be reused for a second forced fight. If every route is dangerous, minimize unsupported forced-fight commitments; among equally committed routes, prefer higher estimated survival, then higher expected ending HP.
- Elite encounters use a three-encounter Act pool and cannot repeat the immediately previous Elite. Use elite_rotation only to remove that one impossible encounter; it does not identify the next Elite exactly.
- PERSISTED RUN STATE run_construction is the latest deterministic CardPicker snapshot. Its modules and deficits are the reviewed pre-action state; confirmed_selection records the resulting choice and its structural progress. It describes growth needs, not proof that a route is survivable. If absent, reason from the explicit deck and encounter evidence instead.
- A Shop converts resources; it is not recovery or automatic growth. Its economic value is limited by the spendable gold expected on arrival, while an unfunded Shop still has the safety value of a room without combat.
- Normally count at most one funded Shop per Act as a primary conversion opportunity. Additional or unfunded Shops add no economic growth, but never reject an otherwise safer route solely because it passes them.
- Treat current gold as evidence and future gold as uncertain. Never rely on a discount or unknown inventory. Unknown inventory alone is not a reason to avoid a funded Shop.
- Treat unspent gold as unrealized power. When a funded Shop is reachable, compare that conversion opportunity against the concrete HP and reward value of extra combats or Elites; do not call a route safer merely because its Rest is early when it contains at least as many risky rooms before that Rest.
